Privacy Policy for LOGOS QUEST

Last updated: November 2026

LOGOS QUEST is an educational and competitive Bible quiz platform designed to encourage scripture learning.

Information We Collect

When participating in quizzes, we may collect basic details such as your Name, Mobile Number, Town, and State to identify participants, manage quiz attempts, and display leaderboard rankings.

How We Use Information

The collected information is solely used for evaluating exam submissions, generating scorecards, displaying live leaderboards, and announcing winners. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third-party marketing services.

Data Security

All exam data and student entries are securely transmitted and processed using encrypted connections via Supabase infrastructure.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or data removal requests regarding LOGOS QUEST, please contact us at: support@logosquest.app
